01

Business Problem

Agricultural trade is fragmented after the crop leaves the farm.

Farmers and buyers often coordinate produce, pricing, storage, and delivery through disconnected channels. That fragmentation makes commercial decisions slower, records harder to trace, and available services more difficult to discover.

Limited market access

Growers need a structured way to publish available produce, while buyers need consistent discovery by crop, grade, origin, quantity, and price.

Weak price visibility

Scattered reference prices make comparison difficult and reduce confidence when farmers and buyers negotiate commercial terms.

Post-harvest coordination

Cold-storage capacity and transport availability are separate operational problems, increasing the risk of delays and product deterioration.

Low workflow traceability

Informal messages and manual follow-ups provide limited visibility into offers, orders, bookings, delivery stages, and responsibility.

Core opportunity

Bring discovery, negotiation, storage, transport, and accountable status records into one locally relevant platform—without pretending that unconfigured payment or external delivery services already exist.

Challenges faced

1

Designing for five distinct roles

Each role required focused permissions and dashboards without fragmenting the experience into separate products.

2

Maintaining trustworthy workflow state

Offers, orders, storage bookings, and transport requests needed controlled transitions and permanent history.

3

Protecting ownership and actions

Role checks, record ownership, prepared statements, CSRF protection, secure sessions, and output escaping had to work together.

4

Keeping one coherent design language

Public pages, authentication, role dashboards, responsive navigation, alerts, and dense operational tables needed one professional system.

5

Aligning schema and migrations

A growing normalized database required careful reconciliation between fresh-install SQL and incremental migrations.

6

Respecting production boundaries

The local MVP had to remain honest about unconfigured external support, password delivery, live market data, and regulated payments.

Project outcome

A unified, responsive agricultural commerce application with role-based dashboards, secure transactional workflows, local support, notifications, market-price records, storage discovery, and transport coordination—built as one maintainable PHP/MySQL codebase.
